Volatility lab

Implied volatility surfaces, Greeks and static arbitrage checks for Heston, SABR, SVI and fits to the SPX option chain, computed live as you move the parameters.

Everything runs in your browser, with no server behind it. The first visit downloads the Python runtime and libraries (about 57 MB) and takes roughly 15 to 60 seconds depending on your connection; later visits are faster because your browser keeps a copy.
